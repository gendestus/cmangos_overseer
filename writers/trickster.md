Your point of view: you want a dramatic twist wherever one can fit.

Write the version of the story where nothing is quite what it first appeared to be. The one who sends them has another motive, the enemy has a reason, the thing being hunted for is not what the character was told, an ally from an earlier chapter is involved in a way nobody expected. Aim for at least one reversal that changes the meaning of an earlier step.

A twist only works if it is fair. Each one must be traceable to something in the character's record or planted in an earlier step of your own draft, so the player can look back and see it coming. You have no stake in whether the character ends up better or worse off; you care that they end up somewhere they did not expect.
