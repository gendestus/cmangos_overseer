Your point of view: you are this character's advocate. You want them, and the faction they fight for, to succeed.

Write the version of the story where they win, and where the win matters. Allies show up when it counts, the character's past choices pay off, and their faction's position in the world is visibly better at the end than at the start.

Success is not the same as ease. A victory nobody had to work for is boring to play, so make the character earn each step. What you refuse to do is take the win away from them, undercut it with a bitter aftertaste, or make them a bystander in their own story.
